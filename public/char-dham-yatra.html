Sacred Destinations

Visit All Four Sacred Dhams

Discover the divine origins of holy rivers and ancient shrines nestled high in the Garhwal Himalayas.

Dham 1

Yamunotri

The sacred origin of the Yamuna River. Situated at an altitude of 3,291 meters, home to hot water springs and the revered Yamunotri temple.

Dham 2

Gangotri

One of India's most revered pilgrimage destinations, associated with the origin of Goddess Ganga nestled amidst lush pine forests and alpine peaks.

Dham 3

Kedarnath

The majestic mountain shrine dedicated to Lord Shiva, located at 3,583 meters near the Mandakini river amidst snow-capped peaks.

Dham 4

Badrinath

The holy abode of Lord Vishnu, situated between Nar and Narayana mountain ranges, famous for its colorful facade and Tapt Kund hot springs.

Your Char Dham Journey at a Glance

A seamless 5-Day / 4-Night luxury helicopter itinerary designed for effortless spiritual immersion.

Day 1

Dehradun → Yamunotri

After breakfast, fly by helicopter to Kharsali, the gateway to Yamunotri. Take a pony/palki to Yamunotri Temple for darshan, then return to the hotel for an evening of rest.

Day 2

Yamunotri → Gangotri

Board your helicopter to Harsil, then drive to sacred Gangotri Temple for darshan. Return to Harsil and unwind amid the Himalayan pine beauty.

Day 3

Gangotri → Kedarnath

Fly to Sersi/Phata and take a shuttle helicopter to Kedarnath. Walk or take VIP assistance to the sacred Kedarnath Temple for darshan, then return to your hotel.

Day 4

Kedarnath → Badrinath

Fly to Badrinath for hotel check-in and darshan at the sacred Badrinath Temple. If time permits, visit Mana Village and attend the evening Aarti.

Day 5

After breakfast, fly back to Dehradun by helicopter. Our team will assist with your onward transfer to the airport or railway station, concluding your divine Yatra.

Essential Awareness

Planning a Char Dham Helicopter Yatra? Here's What You Should Know

Weather & Aviation Operations

Helicopter flying in the Himalayas is strictly subject to weather clearance and DGCA aviation rules. Safety is the ultimate priority. Schedules may adjust dynamically in case of bad weather.

Luggage Restrictions

Due to flight payload balancing, passengers are permitted 1 soft bag of maximum 5 kg weight. Excess baggage can be left safely at the Dehradun base hotel.

Medical Fitness Requirements

Kedarnath and Yamunotri lie at high altitudes (over 10,000 ft). Guests with cardiac or severe respiratory conditions should consult their medical practitioner before travel.

Required Documents

Government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar/Passport/Voter ID) and mandatory Char Dham biometric Yatra registration (our team assists with the registration process).
